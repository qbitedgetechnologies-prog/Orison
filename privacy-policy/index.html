Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Orison International General Trading (“Orison”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and retain personal information when you visit our website or contact us about products and services.

Information we may collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, telephone number, enquiry details, and information you choose to share when requesting product details, a quotation, a repair, or wholesale supply. Basic technical information may also be processed by the website or its hosting provider.

How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, provide product information and quotations, manage customer requests, coordinate purchases or repair services, maintain business records, improve our website, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Sharing information

We do not sell personal information. We may share relevant information with service providers who help operate our website or business, where needed to fulfil your request, or where disclosure is required or permitted by law. We aim to limit sharing to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

WhatsApp and third-party services

If you contact us through WhatsApp or another third-party platform, that provider may process your information under its own terms and privacy policy. Please review the relevant provider’s privacy information.

Security and retention

We take reasonable measures designed to protect personal information. No electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, including applicable business, legal and record-keeping requirements.

Your requests

Subject to applicable law, you may contact us to request access to or correction of personal information, or to raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity before responding.
