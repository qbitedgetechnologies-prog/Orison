Refund and Return Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Orison International General Trading aims to provide clear product information and reliable service. This policy explains how to contact us about a product return, refund request, or concern about a repair. It does not limit any mandatory rights that customers have under applicable UAE law.

How to raise a request

Contact us using the details below and provide your name, purchase or service details (if available), a description of the issue, and supporting photographs or documents where relevant. We will review the request and explain the next steps.

Damaged, defective or incorrectly described items

If an item is damaged, defective, or materially different from the agreed description, please contact us promptly. We will assess the circumstances and provide an appropriate remedy in accordance with applicable law and the terms of the transaction.

New and pre-owned products

Product condition, model, specifications, included accessories and any known limitations should be confirmed at the time of purchase. Any product-specific warranty or return terms will be communicated as applicable. Nothing in this policy excludes mandatory legal rights.

Repair services

If you have a concern about a repair, contact us with the service details and a description of the issue. We will review the matter against the agreed repair scope, applicable service terms and relevant legal requirements.

Refund method and timing

Where a refund is approved or legally required, we will confirm the amount, method and expected processing timeframe. Where practicable, refunds will be made using the original payment method, subject to the circumstances and applicable requirements.
